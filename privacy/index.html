Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

This policy explains what happens to information when you use GNT Downloader at gnt-downloader.pages.dev.

Information we do not collect

There are no accounts or signups. We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to use the downloader.

The links you paste

When you press Download, the link you pasted is sent to a third-party processing service so that it can find the available media files. The file itself is then fetched through that service and saved to your device. Please do not paste links that contain private or sensitive information.

Data stored in your browser

Your theme choice and a list of up to five recent downloads (title, thumbnail link and file link) are saved in your browser’s local storage. This data stays on your device. You can remove the list with the “Clear all” button or by clearing your browser data.

Third-party services

  • Cloudflare Pages hosts this website and may keep standard server logs such as IP address and browser type.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr provide fonts and icons, so your browser contacts those services when the page loads.
  • Media processing service: the Cloudflare Workers service mentioned above that parses links and delivers files.
  • Google Search Console is used by the site owner to monitor search performance. It does not place anything on your device.

Cookies

This site does not set its own cookies and does not run advertising or analytics scripts.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will be updated.
