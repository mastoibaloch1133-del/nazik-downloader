About GNT Downloader

A free, simple tool for saving public videos and audio.

GNT Downloader is a lightweight web tool built by Ghullam Nazik Mastoi. You paste the link of a public video, and the site lists the formats and qualities that are available so you can save the file to your device.

What it does

  • Works in the browser on phone, tablet and desktop. No app and no signup.
  • Supports TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Twitter/X, Snapchat, Pinterest and more.
  • Shows the available qualities and audio-only options when the platform provides them.
  • Keeps a short list of your recent downloads in your own browser, which you can clear anytime.

What it does not do

Only publicly available content can be downloaded. Private, age-restricted or protected videos are not supported, and results depend on what each platform makes available.

Use it responsibly

Please download only content you own or have permission to save, and respect the rights of creators. Read our Terms & DMCA page for details.
